WHY MUNCHIES

Built for how Soshanguve actually orders, not copied from an app built somewhere else.

QR-verified handover

Every pickup and delivery is confirmed with a scannable code, so your order only changes hands with the right person. No "the driver says he delivered it" disputes.

Live WhatsApp updates

Order status lands straight in WhatsApp. No separate app to install or check.

Cash on pickup

Collecting your own order? Pay with cash when you get there, no card required.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE

Watch your order move, in real time

A live map tracks your driver from vendor to your door using real routing, so the ETA you see is the ETA you get, not a guess.

Scan to confirm

A QR code confirms your handover, whether you're collecting yourself or a driver's dropping off.

You won't miss it

A hard-to-miss alert fires when your order's arriving, on top of WhatsApp updates the whole way through.

An order lands, you know immediately

New orders push straight to your dashboard the moment they're paid or confirmed, with an alert loud enough to notice from across the kitchen.

Verified pickup

Show your own QR code when the driver arrives, so there's a record of exactly who collected the order.

Your hours, your call

Set your own operating hours and hit emergency-close in one tap if you need to stop orders right now.

Know exactly what's owed to you

Cash-on-pickup orders are tracked against your account, so settling up is a lookup, not a guess.

A dashboard that finds you the job

Turn-by-turn built in

Scan on both ends
